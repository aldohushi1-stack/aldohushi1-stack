How Marion works out your rates

  1. Capital value × rate in the dollar. Residential: 0.213061 cents per dollar of the Valuer-General’s capital value. $1,000,000 × 0.00213061000 = $2,131.
  2. Minimum rate $1,322. If step 1 comes to less, you pay the minimum. That happens below a capital value of about $620,480.
  3. Plus the Green Adelaide levy — 0.005944 cents in the dollar ($59 per $1m of value), collected for the state.

Business and other land pays a different rate in the dollar: commercial and industrial 0.564611¢; vacant land 0.468734¢; primary production and other 0.213061¢. Pick it in the calculator.

Paying and getting help

  • Pay or check your notice: City of Marion rates page (BPAY, online, direct debit, in person) or call 8375 6600.
  • Struggling to pay: council offers hardship arrangements, and seniors can postpone rates above $500.
  • Concessions: the state Cost of Living Concession (ConcessionsSA 1800 307 758) can go toward rates.
  • Valuation looks wrong: object to the Valuer-General within 60 days.
  • Emergency Services Levy: billed separately — how the ESL works.

Questions about Marion council rates

How much are council rates in Marion?

For 2026/27, a house valued at $1,002,500 — about the typical house sale in the City of Marion — pays about $2,136 in general rates plus $60 Green Adelaide levy, so about $2,196 a year, or $549 a quarter.

What is the Marion council rate in the dollar for 2026/27?

0.213061 cents in the dollar of capital value for residential land, down from 0.224988 in 2025/26. Property values rose, so the rate in the dollar fell; the average residential rate rose 4.80%.

What is the minimum rate in Marion?

$1,322 for 2026/27 (was $1,261). Any residential property with a capital value under about $620,480 pays the minimum.

When are Marion council rates due?

In four instalments, in September, December, March and June. The exact dates are on your rates notice; you can also pay the year in one go.

Does my rates notice include the Emergency Services Levy?

No. The ESL is a state levy billed separately by RevenueSA. The Green Adelaide landscape levy is on your council notice, but council only collects it for the state.

What if I think my valuation is too high?

Object to the Valuer-General in writing within 60 days of receiving your first rates notice for the year — there is no fee. Objecting does not change the due date — keep paying while it is reviewed.

Is there a cap on Marion rate rises?

Marion’s Residential Rate Rebate pays back general rate rises above 10% on a principal place of residence — at least $20 and at most $200 — unless the property had over $20,000 of improvements, a zoning or land-use change, or was sold since 1 January last year.

Thinking of selling or splitting?

Rates follow value. House prices across the City of Marion, check if your block can be split, or ask for a free appraisal.
