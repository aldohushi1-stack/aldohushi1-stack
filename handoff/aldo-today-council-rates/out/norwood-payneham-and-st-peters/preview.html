How Norwood Payneham & St Peters works out your rates

  1. Capital value × rate in the dollar. Residential: 0.18287 cents per dollar of the Valuer-General’s capital value. $1,000,000 × 0.00182870000 = $1,829.
  2. Minimum rate $1,386. If step 1 comes to less, you pay the minimum. That happens below a capital value of about $757,915.
  3. Plus the Green Adelaide levy — 0.006035 cents in the dollar ($60 per $1m of value), collected for the state.

Business and other land pays a different rate in the dollar: commercial, industrial and vacant land 0.23773¢; primary production and other 0.21944¢. Pick it in the calculator.

Paying and getting help

  • Pay or check your notice: City of Norwood Payneham & St Peters rates page (BPAY, online, direct debit, in person) or call 8366 4555.
  • Struggling to pay: council offers hardship arrangements, and seniors can postpone rates above $500.
  • Concessions: the state Cost of Living Concession (ConcessionsSA 1800 307 758) can go toward rates.
  • Valuation looks wrong: object to the Valuer-General within 60 days.
  • Emergency Services Levy: billed separately — how the ESL works.

Questions about Norwood Payneham & St Peters council rates

How much are council rates in Norwood Payneham & St Peters?

For 2026/27, a house valued at $1,530,000 — about the typical house sale in the City of Norwood Payneham & St Peters — pays about $2,798 in general rates plus $92 Green Adelaide levy, so about $2,890 a year, or $723 a quarter. Council’s own average residential rate is $2,356.

What is the Norwood Payneham & St Peters council rate in the dollar for 2026/27?

0.18287 cents in the dollar of capital value for residential land. the average residential rate rose 5.37%.

What is the minimum rate in Norwood Payneham & St Peters?

$1,386 for 2026/27 (was $1,386). Any residential property with a capital value under about $757,915 pays the minimum — 27% of rateable properties.

When are Norwood Payneham & St Peters council rates due?

In four instalments, in September, December, March and June. The exact dates are on your rates notice; you can also pay the year in one go.

Does my rates notice include the Emergency Services Levy?

No. The ESL is a state levy billed separately by RevenueSA. The Green Adelaide landscape levy is on your council notice, but council only collects it for the state.

What if I think my valuation is too high?

Object to the Valuer-General in writing within 60 days of receiving your first rates notice for the year — there is no fee. Objecting does not change the due date — keep paying while it is reviewed.

Is there a cap on Norwood Payneham & St Peters rate rises?

Rises above 15.8% are capped automatically for a principal place of residence (not where the rise comes from improvements, or a change of ownership, land use or zoning).
