How Charles Sturt works out your rates

  1. Capital value × rate in the dollar. Residential: 0.170947733 cents per dollar of the Valuer-General’s capital value. $1,000,000 × 0.00170947733 = $1,709.
  2. Minimum rate $1,410. If step 1 comes to less, you pay the minimum. That happens below a capital value of about $824,814.
  3. Plus the Green Adelaide levy — 0.00588 cents in the dollar ($59 per $1m of value), collected for the state.

Business and other land pays a different rate in the dollar: commercial (shop, office and other) 0.644786739¢; industry (light and other) 0.653323037¢; primary production 0.550338983¢; vacant land 0.530469271¢; other 0.328777377¢. Pick it in the calculator.

Paying and getting help

  • Pay or check your notice: City of Charles Sturt rates page (BPAY, online, direct debit, in person) or call 8408 1111.
  • Struggling to pay: council offers hardship arrangements, and seniors can postpone rates above $500.
  • Concessions: the state Cost of Living Concession (ConcessionsSA 1800 307 758) can go toward rates.
  • Valuation looks wrong: object to the Valuer-General within 60 days.
  • Emergency Services Levy: billed separately — how the ESL works.

Questions about Charles Sturt council rates

How much are council rates in Charles Sturt?

For 2026/27, a house valued at $1,196,500 — about the typical house sale in the City of Charles Sturt — pays about $2,045 in general rates plus $70 Green Adelaide levy, so about $2,116 a year, or $529 a quarter. Council’s own average residential rate is $1,847.

What is the Charles Sturt council rate in the dollar for 2026/27?

0.170947733 cents in the dollar of capital value for residential land, down from 0.181141064 in 2025/26. Property values rose, so the rate in the dollar fell; the average residential rate rose 4.50%.

What is the minimum rate in Charles Sturt?

$1,410 for 2026/27 (was $1,350). Any residential property with a capital value under about $824,814 pays the minimum — 34% of rateable properties.

When are Charles Sturt council rates due?

In four instalments, in September, December, March and June. The exact dates are on your rates notice; you can also pay the year in one go.

Does my rates notice include the Emergency Services Levy?

No. The ESL is a state levy billed separately by RevenueSA. The Green Adelaide landscape levy is on your council notice, but council only collects it for the state.

What if I think my valuation is too high?

Object to the Valuer-General in writing within 60 days of receiving your first rates notice for the year — there is no fee. Objecting does not change the due date — keep paying while it is reviewed.

Is there a cap on Charles Sturt rate rises?

Rate-capping rebate: if your general rates rise more than 12.5% on 2025/26, Council rebates the excess. Not for commercial, industrial, primary production, vacant or other land, or where the rise comes from more than $20,000 of improvements.

Thinking of selling or splitting?

Rates follow value. House prices across the City of Charles Sturt, check if your block can be split, or ask for a free appraisal.
