Rates calculator



How West Torrens works out your rates

  1. Capital value × rate in the dollar. Residential: 0.171528 cents per dollar of the Valuer-General’s capital value. $1,000,000 × 0.00171528000 = $1,715.
  2. Minimum rate $1,258. If step 1 comes to less, you pay the minimum. That happens below a capital value of about $733,408.
  3. Plus the Green Adelaide levy — 0.005691 cents in the dollar ($57 per $1m of value), collected for the state.

Business and other land pays a different rate in the dollar: commercial, industrial, primary production, vacant and other 0.497435¢. Pick it in the calculator.

Paying and getting help

  • Pay or check your notice: City of West Torrens rates page (BPAY, online, direct debit, in person) or call 8416 6333.
  • Struggling to pay: council offers hardship arrangements, and seniors can postpone rates above $500.
  • Concessions: the state Cost of Living Concession (ConcessionsSA 1800 307 758) can go toward rates.
  • Valuation looks wrong: object to the Valuer-General within 60 days.
  • Emergency Services Levy: billed separately — how the ESL works.

Questions about West Torrens council rates

How much are council rates in West Torrens?

For 2026/27, a house valued at $1,180,000 — about the typical house sale in the City of West Torrens — pays about $2,024 in general rates plus $67 Green Adelaide levy, so about $2,091 a year, or $523 a quarter.

What is the West Torrens council rate in the dollar for 2026/27?

0.171528 cents in the dollar of capital value for residential land, down from 0.186838 in 2025/26. Property values rose, so the rate in the dollar fell.

What is the minimum rate in West Torrens?

$1,258 for 2026/27 (was $1,206). Any residential property with a capital value under about $733,408 pays the minimum.

When are West Torrens council rates due?

In four instalments, in September, December, March and June. The exact dates are on your rates notice; you can also pay the year in one go.

Does my rates notice include the Emergency Services Levy?

No. The ESL is a state levy billed separately by RevenueSA. The Green Adelaide landscape levy is on your council notice, but council only collects it for the state.

What if I think my valuation is too high?

Object to the Valuer-General in writing within 60 days of receiving your first rates notice for the year — there is no fee. Objecting does not change the due date — keep paying while it is reviewed.

Is there a cap on West Torrens rate rises?

No. West Torrens resolved not to cap rate rises on principal places of residence for 2026/27 — ask council about hardship options if yours jumps.

Thinking of selling or splitting?

Rates follow value. House prices across the City of West Torrens, check if your block can be split, or ask for a free appraisal.
