How Adelaide Hills works out your rates

  1. Capital value × rate in the dollar. Residential: 0.1955 cents per dollar of the Valuer-General’s capital value. $1,000,000 × 0.00195500000 = $1,955.
  2. Plus a fixed charge of $940, the same for every property.
  3. Plus the Hills and Fleurieu levy — 0.0088 cents in the dollar ($88 per $1m of value), collected for the state.

Business and other land pays a different rate in the dollar: commercial and light industry 0.2658¢; other industry 0.315¢; primary production 0.1958¢; vacant land (neighbourhood zone) 0.256¢; vacant land (other zones) 0.2166¢; other 0.1969¢. Pick it in the calculator.

Paying and getting help

  • Pay or check your notice: Adelaide Hills Council rates page (BPAY, online, direct debit, in person) or call 8408 0400.
  • Struggling to pay: council offers hardship arrangements, and seniors can postpone rates above $500.
  • Concessions: the state Cost of Living Concession (ConcessionsSA 1800 307 758) can go toward rates.
  • Valuation looks wrong: object to the Valuer-General within 60 days.
  • Emergency Services Levy: billed separately — how the ESL works.

Questions about Adelaide Hills council rates

How much are council rates in Adelaide Hills?

For 2026/27, a house valued at $1,060,000 — about the typical house sale in the Adelaide Hills Council — pays about $3,012 in general rates plus $93 Hills and Fleurieu levy, so about $3,106 a year, or $776 a quarter. Council’s own average residential rate is $3,006.

What is the Adelaide Hills council rate in the dollar for 2026/27?

0.1955 cents in the dollar of capital value for residential land. the average residential rate rose 6.80%.

Is there a fixed charge on Adelaide Hills rates?

Yes: every property pays a $940 fixed charge on top of capital value × the rate in the dollar.

When are Adelaide Hills council rates due?

In four instalments, in September, December, March and June. The exact dates are on your rates notice; you can also pay the year in one go.

Does my rates notice include the Emergency Services Levy?

No. The ESL is a state levy billed separately by RevenueSA. The Hills and Fleurieu landscape levy is on your council notice, but council only collects it for the state.

What if I think my valuation is too high?

Object to the Valuer-General in writing within 60 days of receiving your first rates notice for the year — there is no fee. Objecting does not change the due date — keep paying while it is reviewed.

Is there a cap on Adelaide Hills rate rises?

Residential and primary production general rate rises of 15% or more are capped at 15% by an automatic rebate (not for new development, a land-use change, a non-family change of owner, or a newly applied fixed charge).

Thinking of selling or splitting?

Rates follow value. House prices across the Adelaide Hills Council, check if your block can be split, or ask for a free appraisal.
