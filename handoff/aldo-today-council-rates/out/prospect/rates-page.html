Rates calculator



How Prospect works out your rates

  1. Capital value × rate in the dollar. Residential: 0.194523 cents per dollar of the Valuer-General’s capital value. $1,000,000 × 0.00194523000 = $1,945.
  2. Minimum rate $1,610. If step 1 comes to less, you pay the minimum. That happens below a capital value of about $827,666.
  3. Plus the Green Adelaide levy — 0.005722 cents in the dollar ($57 per $1m of value), collected for the state.

Business and other land pays a different rate in the dollar: non-residential 0.533351¢; vacant land (residential zone) 0.58357¢; vacant land (non-residential zone) 1.733392¢. Pick it in the calculator.

Paying and getting help

  • Pay or check your notice: City of Prospect rates page (BPAY, online, direct debit, in person) or call 8269 5355.
  • Struggling to pay: council offers hardship arrangements, and seniors can postpone rates above $500.
  • Concessions: the state Cost of Living Concession (ConcessionsSA 1800 307 758) can go toward rates.
  • Valuation looks wrong: object to the Valuer-General within 60 days.
  • Emergency Services Levy: billed separately — how the ESL works.

Questions about Prospect council rates

How much are council rates in Prospect?

For 2026/27, a house valued at $1,505,000 — about the typical house sale in the City of Prospect — pays about $2,928 in general rates plus $86 Green Adelaide levy, so about $3,014 a year, or $753 a quarter.

What is the Prospect council rate in the dollar for 2026/27?

0.194523 cents in the dollar of capital value for residential land.

What is the minimum rate in Prospect?

$1,610 for 2026/27. Any residential property with a capital value under about $827,666 pays the minimum.

When are Prospect council rates due?

In four instalments, in September, December, March and June. The exact dates are on your rates notice; you can also pay the year in one go.

Does my rates notice include the Emergency Services Levy?

No. The ESL is a state levy billed separately by RevenueSA. The Green Adelaide landscape levy is on your council notice, but council only collects it for the state.

What if I think my valuation is too high?

Object to the Valuer-General in writing within 60 days of receiving your first rates notice for the year — there is no fee. Objecting does not change the due date — keep paying while it is reviewed.

Is there a cap on Prospect rate rises?

Not mentioned on Prospect’s 2026/27 rates page — ask council about hardship options if your rates jump.

Thinking of selling or splitting?

Rates follow value. House prices across the City of Prospect, check if your block can be split, or ask for a free appraisal.
