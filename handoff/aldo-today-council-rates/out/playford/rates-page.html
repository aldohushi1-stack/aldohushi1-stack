How Playford works out your rates

  1. Capital value × rate in the dollar. Residential: 0.126763 cents per dollar of the Valuer-General’s capital value. $1,000,000 × 0.00126763000 = $1,268.
  2. Plus a fixed charge of $1,304, the same for every property.
  3. Plus the Green Adelaide levy — 0.005579 cents in the dollar ($56 per $1m of value), collected for the state.

Business and other land pays a different rate in the dollar: commercial and industrial 1.034459¢; primary production, vacant and other 0.126763¢. Pick it in the calculator.

Paying and getting help

  • Pay or check your notice: City of Playford rates page (BPAY, online, direct debit, in person) or call 8256 0333.
  • Struggling to pay: council offers hardship arrangements, and seniors can postpone rates above $500.
  • Concessions: the state Cost of Living Concession (ConcessionsSA 1800 307 758) can go toward rates.
  • Valuation looks wrong: object to the Valuer-General within 60 days.
  • Emergency Services Levy: billed separately — how the ESL works.

Questions about Playford council rates

How much are council rates in Playford?

For 2026/27, a house valued at $718,500 — about the typical house sale in the City of Playford — pays about $2,215 in general rates plus $40 Green Adelaide levy, so about $2,255 a year, or $564 a quarter.

What is the Playford council rate in the dollar for 2026/27?

0.126763 cents in the dollar of capital value for residential land, down from 0.134944 in 2025/26. Property values rose, so the rate in the dollar fell.

Is there a fixed charge on Playford rates?

Yes: every property pays a $1,304 fixed charge on top of capital value × the rate in the dollar.

When are Playford council rates due?

In four instalments, in September, December, March and June. The exact dates are on your rates notice; you can also pay the year in one go.

Does my rates notice include the Emergency Services Levy?

No. The ESL is a state levy billed separately by RevenueSA. The Green Adelaide landscape levy is on your council notice, but council only collects it for the state.

What if I think my valuation is too high?

Object to the Valuer-General in writing within 60 days of receiving your first rates notice for the year — there is no fee. Objecting does not change the due date — keep paying while it is reviewed.

Is there a cap on Playford rate rises?

No. Playford resolved not to cap rate rises on principal places of residence for 2026/27.

Thinking of selling or splitting?

Rates follow value. House prices across the City of Playford, check if your block can be split, or ask for a free appraisal.
