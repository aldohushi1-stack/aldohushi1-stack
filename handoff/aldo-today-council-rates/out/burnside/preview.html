Rates calculator



How Burnside works out your rates

  1. Capital value × rate in the dollar. Residential: 0.16292 cents per dollar of the Valuer-General’s capital value. $1,000,000 × 0.00162920000 = $1,629.
  2. Minimum rate $1,189. If step 1 comes to less, you pay the minimum. That happens below a capital value of about $729,806.
  3. Plus the Green Adelaide levy — 0.005892 cents in the dollar ($59 per $1m of value), collected for the state.

Business and other land pays a different rate in the dollar: commercial, industrial, primary production and other 0.16292¢; vacant land 0.4073¢; contiguous land 0.4073¢. Pick it in the calculator.

Paying and getting help

  • Pay or check your notice: City of Burnside rates page (BPAY, online, direct debit, in person) or call 8366 4200.
  • Struggling to pay: council offers hardship arrangements, and seniors can postpone rates above $500.
  • Concessions: the state Cost of Living Concession (ConcessionsSA 1800 307 758) can go toward rates.
  • Valuation looks wrong: object to the Valuer-General within 60 days.
  • Emergency Services Levy: billed separately — how the ESL works.

Questions about Burnside council rates

How much are council rates in Burnside?

For 2026/27, a house valued at $1,970,000 — about the typical house sale in the City of Burnside — pays about $3,210 in general rates plus $116 Green Adelaide levy, so about $3,326 a year, or $831 a quarter.

What is the Burnside council rate in the dollar for 2026/27?

0.16292 cents in the dollar of capital value for residential land, down from 0.16855 in 2025/26. Property values rose, so the rate in the dollar fell.

What is the minimum rate in Burnside?

$1,189 for 2026/27 (was $1,125). Any residential property with a capital value under about $729,806 pays the minimum — 18% of rateable properties.

When are Burnside council rates due?

In four instalments, in September, December, March and June. The exact dates are on your rates notice; you can also pay the year in one go.

Does my rates notice include the Emergency Services Levy?

No. The ESL is a state levy billed separately by RevenueSA. The Green Adelaide landscape levy is on your council notice, but council only collects it for the state.

What if I think my valuation is too high?

Object to the Valuer-General in writing within 60 days of receiving your first rates notice for the year — there is no fee. Objecting does not change the due date — keep paying while it is reviewed.

Is there a cap on Burnside rate rises?

Burnside applies a residential rate-cap rebate automatically on the first instalment notice (not for rises from improvements or a change of land use or ownership); the 2026/27 budget doesn't state the threshold, so ask council.

Thinking of selling or splitting?

Rates follow value. House prices across the City of Burnside, check if your block can be split, or ask for a free appraisal.
