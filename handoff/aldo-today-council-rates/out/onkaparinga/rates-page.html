How Onkaparinga works out your rates

  1. Capital value × rate in the dollar. Residential: 0.162861 cents per dollar of the Valuer-General’s capital value. $1,000,000 × 0.00162861000 = $1,629.
  2. Plus a fixed charge of $820, the same for every property.
  3. Plus the Hills and Fleurieu levy — 0.009432057 cents in the dollar ($94 per $1m of value), collected for the state. Properties in the northern, metropolitan part of the council pay the Green Adelaide levy instead (0.006050451¢ in the dollar), about $34 less per $1m.

Business and other land pays a different rate in the dollar: commercial and industrial 0.289584¢; primary production 0.245596¢; vacant land 0.185698¢; other 0.14763¢. Pick it in the calculator.

Paying and getting help

  • Pay or check your notice: City of Onkaparinga rates page (BPAY, online, direct debit, in person) or call 8384 0666.
  • Struggling to pay: council offers hardship arrangements, and seniors can postpone rates above $500.
  • Concessions: the state Cost of Living Concession (ConcessionsSA 1800 307 758) can go toward rates.
  • Valuation looks wrong: object to the Valuer-General within 60 days.
  • Emergency Services Levy: billed separately — how the ESL works.

Questions about Onkaparinga council rates

How much are council rates in Onkaparinga?

For 2026/27, a house valued at $870,000 — about the typical house sale in the City of Onkaparinga — pays about $2,237 in general rates plus $82 Hills and Fleurieu levy, so about $2,319 a year, or $580 a quarter. Council’s own average residential rate is $2,045.

What is the Onkaparinga council rate in the dollar for 2026/27?

0.162861 cents in the dollar of capital value for residential land. the average residential rate rose 4.90%.

Is there a fixed charge on Onkaparinga rates?

Yes: every property pays a $820 fixed charge on top of capital value × the rate in the dollar.

When are Onkaparinga council rates due?

In four instalments, in September, December, March and June. The exact dates are on your rates notice; you can also pay the year in one go.

Does my rates notice include the Emergency Services Levy?

No. The ESL is a state levy billed separately by RevenueSA. The Hills and Fleurieu landscape levy is on your council notice, but council only collects it for the state.

What if I think my valuation is too high?

Object to the Valuer-General in writing within 60 days of receiving your first rates notice for the year — there is no fee. Objecting does not change the due date — keep paying while it is reviewed.

Is there a cap on Onkaparinga rate rises?

Residential rises in the value-based part of the rate are capped at 10% (apply by 31 August 2026); not for rises from improvements, a land-use change, or a change of owner in the past two years, and not on the fixed charge.

Thinking of selling or splitting?

Rates follow value. House prices across the City of Onkaparinga, check if your block can be split, or ask for a free appraisal.
