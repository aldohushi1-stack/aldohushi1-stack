How Campbelltown works out your rates

  1. Capital value × rate in the dollar. Residential: 0.20582 cents per dollar of the Valuer-General’s capital value. $1,000,000 × 0.00205820000 = $2,058.
  2. Minimum rate $1,239. If step 1 comes to less, you pay the minimum. That happens below a capital value of about $601,982.
  3. Plus the Green Adelaide levy — 0.00587 cents in the dollar ($59 per $1m of value), collected for the state. Campbelltown doesn’t publish the levy rate, so this is estimated from its $1,546,800 levy total and the total value of rateable property — within about $1 a year on a typical home. Your rates notice has the exact figure.

Business and other land pays a different rate in the dollar: all other land uses (single rate) 0.20582¢. Pick it in the calculator.

Paying and getting help

  • Pay or check your notice: Campbelltown City Council rates page (BPAY, online, direct debit, in person) or call 8366 9222.
  • Struggling to pay: council offers hardship arrangements, and seniors can postpone rates above $500.
  • Concessions: the state Cost of Living Concession (ConcessionsSA 1800 307 758) can go toward rates.
  • Valuation looks wrong: object to the Valuer-General within 60 days.
  • Emergency Services Levy: billed separately — how the ESL works.

Questions about Campbelltown council rates

How much are council rates in Campbelltown?

For 2026/27, a house valued at $1,210,000 — about the typical house sale in the Campbelltown City Council — pays about $2,490 in general rates plus $71 Green Adelaide levy, so about $2,561 a year, or $640 a quarter.

What is the Campbelltown council rate in the dollar for 2026/27?

0.20582 cents in the dollar of capital value for residential land, down from 0.217122 in 2025/26. Property values rose, so the rate in the dollar fell; the average residential rate rose 3.30%.

What is the minimum rate in Campbelltown?

$1,239 for 2026/27 (was $1,200). Any residential property with a capital value under about $601,982 pays the minimum — 12% of rateable properties.

When are Campbelltown council rates due?

In four instalments, in September, December, March and June. The exact dates are on your rates notice; you can also pay the year in one go.

Does my rates notice include the Emergency Services Levy?

No. The ESL is a state levy billed separately by RevenueSA. The Green Adelaide landscape levy is on your council notice, but council only collects it for the state.

What if I think my valuation is too high?

Object to the Valuer-General in writing within 60 days of receiving your first rates notice for the year — there is no fee. Objecting does not change the due date — keep paying while it is reviewed.

Is there a cap on Campbelltown rate rises?

Rises on a principal place of residence are capped at 6.6% for 2026/27, applied automatically where the property has had no change of owner, land use or improvements since 1 July 2025.

Thinking of selling or splitting?

Rates follow value. House prices across the Campbelltown City Council, check if your block can be split, or ask for a free appraisal.
