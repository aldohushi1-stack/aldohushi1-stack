How Holdfast Bay works out your rates

  1. Capital value × rate in the dollar. Residential: 0.1807032 cents per dollar of the Valuer-General’s capital value. $1,000,000 × 0.00180703200 = $1,807.
  2. Minimum rate $1,380. If step 1 comes to less, you pay the minimum. That happens below a capital value of about $763,683.
  3. Plus the Green Adelaide levy — 0.0060478 cents in the dollar ($60 per $1m of value), collected for the state.

Business and other land pays a different rate in the dollar: commercial, industrial and vacant land 0.30221¢. Pick it in the calculator.

Paying and getting help

  • Pay or check your notice: City of Holdfast Bay rates page (BPAY, online, direct debit, in person) or call 8229 9999.
  • Struggling to pay: council offers hardship arrangements, and seniors can postpone rates above $500.
  • Concessions: the state Cost of Living Concession (ConcessionsSA 1800 307 758) can go toward rates.
  • Valuation looks wrong: object to the Valuer-General within 60 days.
  • Emergency Services Levy: billed separately — how the ESL works.

Questions about Holdfast Bay council rates

How much are council rates in Holdfast Bay?

For 2026/27, a house valued at $1,525,000 — about the typical house sale in the City of Holdfast Bay — pays about $2,756 in general rates plus $92 Green Adelaide levy, so about $2,848 a year, or $712 a quarter.

What is the Holdfast Bay council rate in the dollar for 2026/27?

0.1807032 cents in the dollar of capital value for residential land, down from 0.183 in 2025/26. Property values rose, so the rate in the dollar fell.

What is the minimum rate in Holdfast Bay?

$1,380 for 2026/27 (was $1,295). Any residential property with a capital value under about $763,683 pays the minimum — 26% of rateable properties.

When are Holdfast Bay council rates due?

In four instalments, in September, December, March and June. The exact dates are on your rates notice; you can also pay the year in one go.

Does my rates notice include the Emergency Services Levy?

No. The ESL is a state levy billed separately by RevenueSA. The Green Adelaide landscape levy is on your council notice, but council only collects it for the state.

What if I think my valuation is too high?

Object to the Valuer-General in writing within 60 days of receiving your first rates notice for the year — there is no fee. Objecting does not change the due date — keep paying while it is reviewed.

Is there a cap on Holdfast Bay rate rises?

On application, rises on your principal place of residence are capped at 10% (not after more than $50,000 of improvements in the past two years, a change of land use, zoning or owner).

Thinking of selling or splitting?

Rates follow value. House prices across the City of Holdfast Bay, check if your block can be split, or ask for a free appraisal.
