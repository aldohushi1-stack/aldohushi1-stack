How Mitcham works out your rates

  1. Capital value × rate in the dollar. Residential: 0.2094 cents per dollar of the Valuer-General’s capital value. $1,000,000 × 0.00209400000 = $2,094.
  2. Minimum rate $1,536. If step 1 comes to less, you pay the minimum. That happens below a capital value of about $733,524.
  3. Plus the Green Adelaide levy — 0.005988 cents in the dollar ($60 per $1m of value), collected for the state.

Business and other land pays a different rate in the dollar: commercial, industrial and vacant land 0.523291¢; primary production and other 0.2094¢. Pick it in the calculator.

Paying and getting help

  • Pay or check your notice: City of Mitcham rates page (BPAY, online, direct debit, in person) or call 1300 133 466.
  • Struggling to pay: council offers hardship arrangements, and seniors can postpone rates above $500.
  • Concessions: the state Cost of Living Concession (ConcessionsSA 1800 307 758) can go toward rates.
  • Valuation looks wrong: object to the Valuer-General within 60 days.
  • Emergency Services Levy: billed separately — how the ESL works.

Questions about Mitcham council rates

How much are council rates in Mitcham?

For 2026/27, a house valued at $1,319,000 — about the typical house sale in the City of Mitcham — pays about $2,762 in general rates plus $79 Green Adelaide levy, so about $2,841 a year, or $710 a quarter.

What is the Mitcham council rate in the dollar for 2026/27?

0.2094 cents in the dollar of capital value for residential land, down from 0.216367 in 2025/26. Property values rose, so the rate in the dollar fell; the average residential rate rose 6.38%.

What is the minimum rate in Mitcham?

$1,536 for 2026/27 (was $1,444). Any residential property with a capital value under about $733,524 pays the minimum — 14% of rateable properties.

When are Mitcham council rates due?

In four instalments, in September, December, March and June. The exact dates are on your rates notice; you can also pay the year in one go.

Does my rates notice include the Emergency Services Levy?

No. The ESL is a state levy billed separately by RevenueSA. The Green Adelaide landscape levy is on your council notice, but council only collects it for the state.

What if I think my valuation is too high?

Object to the Valuer-General in writing within 60 days of receiving your first rates notice for the year — there is no fee. Objecting does not change the due date — keep paying while it is reviewed.

Is there a cap on Mitcham rate rises?

General rate rises over 12.5% on a principal place of residence are capped, except after more than $25,000 of improvements, a land-use change, a change of owner since 1 July 2025, or newly divided vacant land. Pay in full by 15 September for a 1% discount.

Thinking of selling or splitting?

Rates follow value. House prices across the City of Mitcham, check if your block can be split, or ask for a free appraisal.
