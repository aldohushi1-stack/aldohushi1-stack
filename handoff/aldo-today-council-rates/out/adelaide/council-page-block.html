• Council rates: the City of Adelaide rates on annual value (75% of a year’s rent), not capital value. The average home pays about $2,387 a year — City of Adelaide rates calculator.
    • The City of Adelaide is the only South Australian council that rates on annual value instead of capital value. Annual value is set by the Valuer-General as 75% of the yearly rent the property could earn, with the landlord paying rates, taxes and insurance — whether or not the owner lives there. Council’s own figure for the average home in 2026/27 is $2,387 in general rates, up 5.6%. That matches an annual value of about $19,945 — a home that would rent for about $511 a week. The Green Adelaide levy adds about $31. Work out your own on the City of Adelaide rates calculator: https://aldo.today/councils/adelaide/rates
