How the City of Adelaide works out your rates

  1. Annual value. The Valuer-General sets it at 75% of the yearly rent your property could earn (landlord paying rates, taxes and insurance), whether you rent it out or live in it. A home that would rent for $600 a week has an annual value of about $23,400.
  2. Annual value × rate in the dollar. Residential: 11.967651 cents per dollar. $20,000 × 0.11967651 = $2,394.
  3. Minimum rate $415 — paid if step 2 comes to less (an annual value under about $3,468).
  4. Plus the Green Adelaide levy — 0.154198 cents per dollar of annual value, collected for the state.
  5. Less any rebate: the automatic 10% cap on rises, and $100 off for eligible pensioners.

Business land pays 14.660372¢ and vacant land 23.935301¢ per dollar of annual value. Shops in the Rundle Mall precinct also pay the Rundle Mall separate rate. Short-stay rentals are rated as business.

Paying and getting help

  • Pay or check your notice: City of Adelaide rates page or call 8203 7203.
  • Pensioners: a $100 rebate per household, plus the state Cost of Living Concession (ConcessionsSA 1800 307 758).
  • Valuation looks wrong: object to the Valuer-General within 60 days.
  • Emergency Services Levy: billed separately — how the ESL works.

Questions about City of Adelaide council rates

How much are council rates in the City of Adelaide?

Council’s own figure for the average home in 2026/27 is $2,387 in general rates, up 5.6%. That matches an annual value of about $19,945 — a home that would rent for about $511 a week. The Green Adelaide levy adds about $31.

Why are City of Adelaide rates based on rent, not property value?

The City of Adelaide is the only South Australian council that rates on annual value instead of capital value. Annual value is set by the Valuer-General as 75% of the yearly rent the property could earn, with the landlord paying rates, taxes and insurance — whether or not the owner lives there.

What is the City of Adelaide rate in the dollar for 2026/27?

11.967651 cents per dollar of annual value for residential land (was 11.5205 in 2025/26), so a home with an annual value of $20,000 pays $2,394. Non-residential land pays 14.660372 cents and vacant land 23.935301 cents.

What is the minimum rate in the City of Adelaide?

$415 for 2026/27 (was $400), paid by any property with an annual value under about $3,468.

Is there a cap on City of Adelaide rate rises?

Increases in general rates are capped at 10% a year, applied automatically (not where the rise comes from improvements, or a change of land use, ownership or occupancy). Pensioners get a $100 rebate.

When are City of Adelaide rates due?

In four instalments: 1 September 2026, 1 December 2026, 1 March 2027 and 1 June 2027.

Does my rates notice include the Emergency Services Levy?

No. The ESL is billed separately by RevenueSA. The Green Adelaide levy is on your council notice, but council only collects it for the state.

What if I think my annual value is too high?

Object to the Valuer-General in writing within 60 days of receiving your first rates notice for the year — there is no fee. Keep paying while it is reviewed.

Thinking of selling or renting out?

House prices in the City of Adelaide, or ask for a free appraisal.
