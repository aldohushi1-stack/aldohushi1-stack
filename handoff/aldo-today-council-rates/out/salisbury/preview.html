How Salisbury works out your rates

  1. Capital value × rate in the dollar. Residential: 0.2636 cents per dollar of the Valuer-General’s capital value. $1,000,000 × 0.00263600000 = $2,636.
  2. Minimum rate $1,340. If step 1 comes to less, you pay the minimum. That happens below a capital value of about $508,346.
  3. Less the high-value adjustment for homes (not commercial use or multiple dwellings): 10% off the rates on value $969,000–$1,065,000; 20% off the rates on value $1,065,000–$1,205,000; 35% off the rates on value above $1,205,000.
  4. Plus the Green Adelaide levy — 0.005867 cents in the dollar ($59 per $1m of value), collected for the state.

Business and other land pays a different rate in the dollar: commercial, industrial and marina berth 0.4527¢; vacant land 0.3427¢; primary production and other 0.2636¢. Pick it in the calculator.

Paying and getting help

  • Pay or check your notice: City of Salisbury rates page (BPAY, online, direct debit, in person) or call 8406 8222.
  • Struggling to pay: council offers hardship arrangements, and seniors can postpone rates above $500.
  • Concessions: the state Cost of Living Concession (ConcessionsSA 1800 307 758) can go toward rates.
  • Valuation looks wrong: object to the Valuer-General within 60 days.
  • Emergency Services Levy: billed separately — how the ESL works.

Questions about Salisbury council rates

How much are council rates in Salisbury?

For 2026/27, a house valued at $828,000 — about the typical house sale in the City of Salisbury — pays about $2,183 in general rates plus $49 Green Adelaide levy, so about $2,231 a year, or $558 a quarter. Council’s own average residential rate is $1,843.

What is the Salisbury council rate in the dollar for 2026/27?

0.2636 cents in the dollar of capital value for residential land, down from 0.2744 in 2025/26. Property values rose, so the rate in the dollar fell; the average residential rate rose 4.70%.

What is the minimum rate in Salisbury?

$1,340 for 2026/27 (was $1,280). Any residential property with a capital value under about $508,346 pays the minimum — 14% of rateable properties.

When are Salisbury council rates due?

In four instalments, in September, December, March and June. The exact dates are on your rates notice; you can also pay the year in one go.

Does my rates notice include the Emergency Services Levy?

No. The ESL is a state levy billed separately by RevenueSA. The Green Adelaide landscape levy is on your council notice, but council only collects it for the state.

What if I think my valuation is too high?

Object to the Valuer-General in writing within 60 days of receiving your first rates notice for the year — there is no fee. Objecting does not change the due date — keep paying while it is reviewed.

Is there a cap on Salisbury rate rises?

On application, general rates on a principal place of residence owned by a person are capped at a 12.5% rise on 2025/26 where the rise comes from valuation changes (not improvements, land-use or zoning changes, or a change of owner).

Thinking of selling or splitting?

Rates follow value. House prices across the City of Salisbury, check if your block can be split, or ask for a free appraisal.
