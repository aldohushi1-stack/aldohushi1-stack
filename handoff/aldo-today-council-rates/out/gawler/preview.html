How Gawler works out your rates

  1. Capital value × rate in the dollar. Residential: 0.3331 cents per dollar of the Valuer-General’s capital value. $1,000,000 × 0.00333100000 = $3,331.
  2. Minimum rate $1,320. If step 1 comes to less, you pay the minimum. That happens below a capital value of about $396,277.
  3. Plus a $290 waste service charge on most properties with a building.
  4. Plus the Regional Landscape levy — 0.008406 cents in the dollar ($84 per $1m of value), collected for the state.

This calculator covers homes. Business and other land is rated differently — check with council.

Paying and getting help

  • Pay or check your notice: Town of Gawler rates page (BPAY, online, direct debit, in person) or call 8522 9211.
  • Struggling to pay: council offers hardship arrangements, and seniors can postpone rates above $500.
  • Concessions: the state Cost of Living Concession (ConcessionsSA 1800 307 758) can go toward rates.
  • Valuation looks wrong: object to the Valuer-General within 60 days.
  • Emergency Services Levy: billed separately — how the ESL works.

Questions about Gawler council rates

How much are council rates in Gawler?

For 2026/27, a house valued at $760,000 — about the typical house sale in the Town of Gawler — pays about $2,532 in general rates plus $64 Regional Landscape levy and a $290 waste service charge, so about $2,885 a year, or $721 a quarter.

What is the Gawler council rate in the dollar for 2026/27?

0.3331 cents in the dollar of capital value for residential land, down from 0.3517 in 2025/26. Property values rose, so the rate in the dollar fell.

What is the minimum rate in Gawler?

$1,320 for 2026/27 (was $1,266). Any residential property with a capital value under about $396,277 pays the minimum.

When are Gawler council rates due?

In four instalments, in September, December, March and June. The exact dates are on your rates notice; you can also pay the year in one go.

Does my rates notice include the Emergency Services Levy?

No. The ESL is a state levy billed separately by RevenueSA. The Regional Landscape landscape levy is on your council notice, but council only collects it for the state.

What if I think my valuation is too high?

Object to the Valuer-General in writing within 60 days of receiving your first rates notice for the year — there is no fee. Objecting does not change the due date — keep paying while it is reviewed.

Is there a cap on Gawler rate rises?

On application within 60 days of your first notice, Gawler rebates general rate rises above 10% for self-funded retirees and people mainly on fixed government benefits, and above 20% for other residential ratepayers (excluding the landscape levy and waste charge; eligibility rules in the Strategic Rating Policy).

Thinking of selling or splitting?

Rates follow value. House prices across the Town of Gawler, check if your block can be split, or ask for a free appraisal.
