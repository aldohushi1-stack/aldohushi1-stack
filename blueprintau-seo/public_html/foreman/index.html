What is Foreman? Free quoting, jobs, crew & invoicing for Australian tradies

Free quoting and invoicing software built for Australian trade businesses

Foreman is a free operations console for sole traders and small trade crews — electricians, plumbers, carpenters, tilers, painters, landscapers and anyone else who quotes a job, does the work and then has to chase the money. It replaces the mix of notepad, spreadsheet and accounting app that most tradies run their business on, with one screen that follows a job from quote to paid.

You write the quote from a price book you control, send it, and when it's accepted Foreman turns it into a job. Jobs go onto a weekly crew schedule so you can see who's where. When the work's signed off, the invoice is generated from the same line items — a proper Australian tax invoice with your ABN, GST calculated at 10%, and the inc-GST total your client expects to see. Nothing gets re-typed between the ute and the office.

Why tradies pick Foreman over a paid subscription

Most quoting and invoicing apps for tradies charge $30 to $80 a month per user, and the free tiers cap how many quotes you can send. Foreman is free forever with no user limits and no quote limits. It runs entirely in your browser — your data lives on your own device, you can export the whole workspace to Excel at any time, and you can import your existing clients, crew, price book, jobs and invoices from a spreadsheet to get started in minutes.

Foreman is part of the BlueprintAU Trade Suite. Takeoff measures the job straight off the plan and sends the priced scope to Foreman as quote lines; Ledger takes the paid invoices and shows you who still owes you, where the money's going, and what the BAS will look like. Use one or all three.

Common questions

Is Foreman really free?
Yes. Foreman is free forever, with no user limits, no quote or invoice caps, and no card required. There's an optional tip jar if you want to shout the lab a beer.
Does Foreman handle GST and produce a valid Australian tax invoice?
Yes. Prices are entered ex GST, GST is calculated at 10%, and every invoice carries the words 'Tax invoice', your business name and ABN, the date, an itemised description, and the GST amount and inc-GST total — the elements the ATO requires.
Where is my data stored?
In your own browser on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Export the workspace to Excel to back it up or move it to another computer, and import it back with the same file.
Can I bring in my existing clients and price list?
Yes. Download the Excel template from the welcome screen, fill in clients, crew, price book, jobs and invoices, and import the .xlsx or .csv. You can also start from the loaded demo business to see how it all fits together.
Does it work on a phone or tablet?
Foreman is a web app and runs in any modern browser on desktop, tablet or phone. It's laid out for a screen at least the size of a tablet, where the quote builder and schedule are easiest to use.
What trades is Foreman for?
Any Australian trade that quotes, schedules and invoices: electrical, plumbing, carpentry, tiling, painting, landscaping, fencing, air conditioning, roofing, concreting and general building maintenance.
