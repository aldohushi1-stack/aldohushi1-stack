About water filters

Plain answers, no sales talk. If anything here doesn't match what you're tasting at home, give us a call.

What a filter does

It takes out the chlorine taste

Mains water is treated with chlorine so it stays safe on the long trip to your house. A carbon filter removes the taste and smell at the tap.

It catches grit and rust

Small particles picked up from the pipes — the things that make water look cloudy and wear out tap washers and kettles.

It leaves the good minerals in

Standard filters don't strip out calcium or magnesium. Only the reverse osmosis system does that, which is why we suggest it mainly for tank or bore water.
