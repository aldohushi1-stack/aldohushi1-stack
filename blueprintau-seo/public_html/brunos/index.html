Professor Bruno reads aloud.
Follow the glowing words — or tap any word to play it, like a piano key!
A hand-drawn storybook game about how grown-ups make wine from grapes. Little bears drink grape juice!

Bruno's Play Guide

How grapes become wine, in six little steps. This is how to play, too!

1
Pick the grapes. Grapes grow on vines in the sunshine. When they are sweet and ripe, tap every bunch to pop it into the basket.
2
Squish, squish! Tap the big tub and Bruno stomps the grapes into mushy juice. Winemakers call this juicy mush the must.
3
Press it. Turn the handle and the press gives the grapes a big gentle hug, so every last drop of juice runs out into the jug.
4
Bubble time! The juice rests in a big tank. Tiny helpers called yeast eat the sugar and burp little bubbles. Slowly, the juice turns into wine. Pop the bubbles as they rise!
5
A long nap. The young wine sleeps in wooden barrels in a cool cellar — through spring, summer, autumn and winter. Pat the barrel once for each season. Shhh!
6
Pop the corks in! Tap each bottle to fill it and push in a cork. All done — you are a little winemaker!
For little bears: wine is a special drink for grown-ups only. Bruno celebrates with a big cup of grape juice — it is made from the very same grapes!
Words to know
Must — squished grapes and juice, all mushy together.
Press — a machine that hugs the grapes to squeeze out juice.
Yeast — tiny helpers, too small to see, that turn sweet juice into wine.
Ferment — the bubbly magic the yeast does inside the tank.
Cellar — a cool, quiet room where wine naps in barrels.
Cork — the little stopper that keeps the wine safe in its bottle.
