About Adtrade

Adelaide's industrial supplier, run by people who know the gear

Adtrade is a South Australian industrial supplier that has been servicing Adelaide with an extensive range of ladders, trolleys, wheels and castors since 1999. It was started by Domenico "Dom" Alloca who, after more than 15 years in the industry, decided to set off on his own — bringing hands-on experience in the sale and manufacture of ladders, trolleys and other trade and commercial equipment.

Today Adtrade is expanding its product offering with a bigger warehouse-style retail premise and showroom at 135 South Rd, Thebarton. From here, Dom and the team keep servicing Adelaide with fast, reliable service and a wider range of stock — for trade professionals and the general public alike.

Read the full return policy
  1. Goods must have been purchased directly from Adtrade.
  2. Any goods short supplied or incorrectly supplied must be brought to Adtrade's attention immediately.
  3. A Credit Authorisation Number (CAN) must be obtained from our credit department prior to goods being returned — ring (08) 8354 0011 or email sales@adtrade.com.au.
  4. The CAN must be written on a copy of the original invoice for the goods being returned, and included with the returned items.
  5. Goods returned to Adtrade must have all freight prepaid, unless prior arrangements have been made.
  6. Goods must be complete, in their original packaging and in a saleable condition — no dirty or greasy marks, or transport/packing tape damage — and include all instructions/paperwork originally supplied.
  7. All goods returned will be subject to a minimum 15% restocking fee, unless prior arrangements have been made.
  8. Goods will not be accepted for credit if: returned after 30 days from the invoice date; returned without a CAN; returned without a copy of the invoice; they were specially procured for you; they've been fitted, used, altered or damaged in any way; or the plastic shrink wrap/blister packaging has been opened.
  9. While all care is taken to ensure correct supply, no responsibility is accepted by Adtrade for the fitment of incorrect parts — the onus is on the purchaser to confirm parts suit the application. Any costs from fitting incorrect parts are the fitter's responsibility.

The only exception to this policy: genuine warranty claims as per Australian statutory requirements.
