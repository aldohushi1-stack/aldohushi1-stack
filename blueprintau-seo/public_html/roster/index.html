Is it really free?

Yes. Roster is a working demo from the BlueprintAU lab in Adelaide. Clubs use it free; if it earns its keep we may add paid extras later, but the roster stays free. It's one of a handful of practical tools we build out of the same Adelaide lab for the day-to-day of running a club or a venue.

Do members need an app?

No. The link opens in any phone browser. They type their name and mobile once and it's remembered.

What about reminders?

A confirmation when they sign up, a reminder two days out and another the morning of — by email, and by text where the club has SMS switched on. Timing is adjustable.

Who sees the phone numbers?

Only the coordinator, on the admin page. Members see first names only. Nothing is sold or shared.
