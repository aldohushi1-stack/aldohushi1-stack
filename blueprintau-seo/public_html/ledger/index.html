What is Ledger? Free receivables, expenses & BAS tracking for Australian tradies

Free cash-flow and BAS tracking for Australian trade businesses

Ledger is a free money console for sole traders and small trade crews. It answers the three questions a tradie actually asks about money: who still owes me, where is it all going, and what is the BAS going to cost me this quarter? It is not accounting software and it doesn't try to be — there's no chart of accounts to set up and no bank feed to reconcile. It's the view of your business cash that you'd otherwise build in a spreadsheet at 10pm.

Invoices come in from Foreman (or you enter them here), and Ledger tracks what's paid, what's outstanding and what's overdue. Expenses go in with their GST component. From those two sides it works out your GST collected, GST paid and net GST position on a cash basis — the basis most small trade businesses report on — so the BAS estimate at the top of the screen is the number you'll be handing to the ATO, not a surprise.

Why Ledger instead of a bookkeeping subscription

Cloud accounting for a sole trader runs $30 to $70 a month, and most tradies use a fraction of it. Ledger is free forever, runs in your browser with your data on your own device, and exports everything to Excel so your accountant or bookkeeper gets a clean file at quarter end. If you later grow into full accounting software, the export goes with you.

Common questions

Does Ledger replace my accountant or Xero/MYOB?
No. Ledger is a cash-flow and GST view for the owner, not a general ledger. It gives you the day-to-day numbers and a BAS estimate; your accountant still lodges. Export to Excel at quarter end and hand it over.
Is the BAS estimate on a cash or accruals basis?
Cash basis — GST is counted when money is actually received or paid, which is how most small trade businesses under the turnover threshold report. The figure is an estimate to plan around, not a lodgement.
Is Ledger free?
Yes, free forever. No subscription, no user limits, no card. Your data stays in your browser on your own device.
Does it connect to my bank?
No. Ledger deliberately has no bank feed — you enter or import expenses, and invoices flow in from Foreman. That's what keeps it free and keeps your bank credentials out of a third-party app.
Can I import my existing invoices and expenses?
Yes. Import from an .xlsx or .csv using the template on the welcome screen, or export a Foreman workspace and bring the invoices across.
Who is Ledger for?
Australian sole traders and small trade businesses — electrical, plumbing, building, landscaping, painting and the like — who want to know their cash position and BAS exposure without paying for accounting software they don't use.
