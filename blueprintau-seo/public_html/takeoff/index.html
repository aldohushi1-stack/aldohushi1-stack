What is Takeoff? Free plan measurement & estimating for Australian tradies

Free takeoff software: measure the job off the plan, price it on the spot

Takeoff is a free plan-measurement and estimating tool for Australian tradies and small builders. Drop in a plan as a PDF or image, calibrate the scale once against a known dimension, and then click to measure what the job actually needs: lineal metres of skirting, cable run or pipework; square metres of tiling, paint, plasterboard or turf; counts of downlights, power points, doors or posts.

Every measurement is tied to a rate from your own price book, so the scope is priced as you go — ex GST, GST at 10%, and the inc-GST figure your client will see. When you're done, the priced scope exports as quote line items straight into Foreman, so the quote you send is built from the measurements you took rather than a number you guessed in the car park.

Why a free takeoff tool instead of a paid estimating platform

Construction takeoff and estimating software is usually sold to builders and estimators at $50 to $200 a month, and most of what it does is aimed at commercial tenders. Takeoff is built for the trade doing a bathroom reno, a rewire, a fence line or a landscaping job — where you need accurate quantities fast, not a bill of quantities for a tender. It runs entirely in your browser, there's no account to create, and your plans and measurements stay on your own device.

Common questions

What plan formats can I measure from?
PDF plans and common image formats (PNG, JPG). Drop the file in, pick a dimension you know — a 2,400 mm door opening, a 900 mm wall length — and Takeoff scales the whole sheet from it.
How accurate is a takeoff from a PDF plan?
As accurate as the plan and your calibration. Calibrate against the longest dimension you can find on the sheet; a plan at scale is typically within a percent or two, which is well inside the allowance most trades already carry for waste.
Is Takeoff free?
Yes, free forever. No trial period, no quote limits, no card. Nothing is uploaded — the plan and your measurements stay in your browser.
Does it send the quote for me?
Takeoff produces the priced scope. Export it as quote lines and it drops straight into Foreman, the free quoting and invoicing app in the same suite, which sends the quote and turns it into a job and a tax invoice.
Can I use my own rates?
Yes. Takeoff prices against your own price book — lineal, area and count rates — so a square metre of wall tiling or a downlight install is priced at what you actually charge.
Which trades use plan takeoffs?
Electrical, plumbing, tiling, painting, plastering, carpentry, flooring, landscaping, fencing and small building work — any job where the quantities are on a drawing before they're on site.
