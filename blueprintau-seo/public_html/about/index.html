Why it matters

Software built by someone who knows the gear

Most software for the trades is written by people who have never stood next to the machine it's meant to run around. I've spent twenty years on both sides: the software and the machinery. So when you tell me how a job really moves through your business, I don't need a translator.

That's why BlueprintAU builds one tool for one problem, for a fixed $2,000. Quotes that live in the ute, a roster run from a group chat, a catalogue that's last year's PDF. We name the job in an hour and it's live on your name in five working days.
