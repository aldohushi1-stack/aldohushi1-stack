Week one: starting the diary

This is the first page of a working diary. From here on it's one short entry a night: what got built, what shipped, what broke.

This week needs a catch-up, so here it is.

Glassbox had five releases between Tuesday and Thursday. It's my open-source tool for seeing what AI coding agents actually do. "collect" rolls every machine's report into one fleet view. "fence" finds secrets (keys, tokens, passwords) that ended up in session transcripts and can shred them in place. "adhere" reads a project's CLAUDE.md and checks, line by line, whether the agent followed it. Its first run scored the session that built it at 22%. Then 0.9.1 added a loop guard that stops an agent repeating a tool call that has already failed twice.

Pour is the second "ad for the ad". It's a 30-second playable: hold to pour a beer, let go at the line. Then it turns out to be an ad for BlueprintAU builds.

Two tools for my own bench. Aldo Motion is a motion-graphics editor that runs in the browser and exports MP4, GIF and live HTML. SFTP Deploy pushes a site to the host, shows what will change first, and backs up anything it overwrites.

The AI Answer Monitor is designed. It's the paid follow-on to the Six-Rung Check: every week it asks AI the ten questions a customer would ask about a trade in their area, then reports who got named.

And this site. aldo.today went from a domain sitting unused to live today: this diary out front, and a private desk behind it for the day's list, follow-ups and money.

Tomorrow's entry will be shorter.
